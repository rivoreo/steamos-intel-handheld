SteamOS support for Intel handhelds

SteamOS is built around AMD handhelds, so parts of it do not work on Intel hardware. This adds what is missing: two panels in the Steam menu that manage power while you play and protect the battery, plus the sensor plumbing MangoHud needs to show real numbers.

What this is, in plain terms.

Four questions worth answering before you install anything on your handheld.

What it is

A package plus two panels in the Steam quick access menu: Game Power, which manages power while a game runs, and Charge Limit, which stops charging before the battery is full.

What it can do

Hold your frame rate using less power, drop the frame rate while you are not touching the device, stop charging at 60% or 80% to slow battery ageing, and let MangoHud read real CPU and graphics power.

Why it exists

SteamOS assumes AMD hardware. On an Intel handheld the power controls do not reach the right knobs and MangoHud cannot see the sensors, so the built-in tools report nothing useful and manage power badly.

How to install

One command imports the signing key, adds the package repository and installs everything. Then turn on the two panels in Decky Loader. Details below.

What you get.

Everything here is tested on real hardware before release, and the measurements behind these claims are published in the repository.

Game Power

Holds the frame rate you asked for at the lowest power the scene actually needs, instead of running the chip flat out and hoping. Backs off immediately if frame pacing suffers.

Saves power while you are away

A game left running with nobody holding the device drops to 30 FPS, measured at around 26% less power, and returns to full speed the moment you touch a button.

Charge Limit

Stop charging at 60% or 80%. Keeping a battery off a full charge slows how fast it ages, which matters if the device mostly sits plugged in.

Working power sensors

MangoHud can read real CPU package power and Intel integrated graphics power, so the overlay shows actual watts instead of blanks.

Signed updates

Delivered through a signed pacman repository, so updates arrive the normal way and pacman verifies them against a pinned key.

Tested on the real device

Developed and measured on an MSI Claw 8 AI+. Claims about power and frame pacing come from on-device measurements, not estimates.

Using it.

Both panels live in the Steam quick access menu, under the Decky plug icon.

Game Power

Pick a power profile and, if you want, a frame rate target. Automatic follows whether you are on battery or plugged in. Leave the frame target on Automatic and it follows the limit you already set in Steam.

Charge Limit

Choose 60%, 80%, or charge to full. The setting lives in the hardware, so it keeps working after a reboot and while the device is switched off.

Seeing the numbers

Turn on Steam's performance overlay to watch CPU and graphics power while you play. Each panel also has a technical details toggle, off by default.
